About the chef

Precision up front, payoff at the end.

I've spent my career treating media plans like a good recipe: measure twice, test the method, and never skip a step just because it's slow.

Since 2023, I've moved from running a $5M+, 34-partner offsite portfolio, to building first-ever media strategies for Petco's newest brand partners, to now focusing on the operations and creative infrastructure, like a cross-brand asset bank, that keep every campaign on schedule.

Off the clock, I'm probably actually in a kitchen. Baking is where I first fell in love with the idea that a great outcome is just a well-tested process repeated with care: exact measurements, a method you trust, and the patience to let it prove out. Turns out that's pretty good training for retail media, too.
